# AIIA TrialHub

Design and build a premium production-grade frontend for:

AIIA TrialShield
(Ayurveda Clinical Research & Pharmacovigilance Command Center)

IMPORTANT:

This must NOT look like a generic AI-generated dashboard, SaaS template, admin panel, hospital ERP, CRM, or analytics dashboard.

Avoid:
- Large dark left sidebar
- Generic KPI cards everywhere
- White empty screens
- Template-style admin layouts
- Purple/neon gradients
- Crypto startup aesthetics

The platform should feel like:

- National healthcare institution software
- AIIA research platform
- Modern government-grade digital product
- Clinical research command center
- Premium healthcare operations system
- Apple-level attention to detail
- Calm, trustworthy, sophisticated

VISUAL STYLE

Theme:
Modern Ayurveda + Clinical Research

Color Palette:
Primary: #0B3D2E
Secondary: #1F6F50
Accent: #D4A373
Background: #F8F5EF
Surface: #FFFFFF
Text: #102A22

Typography:
Inter / Geist

Visual inspiration:
- Ayurvedic manuscripts
- Botanical illustrations
- Research journals
- Premium healthcare platforms
- Modern Indian design language

Use subtle:
- leaf patterns
- botanical line art
- manuscript textures
- elegant gradients

Never make it look decorative or spiritual.

Create a world-class landing page with:

Hero Section:
"AIIA TrialShield"

Ayurveda Clinical Research & Pharmacovigilance Command Center

Supporting text:
Monitor clinical trials, ethics readiness, participant safety, pharmacovigilance workflows and compliance risks from a unified platform.

Background:
Elegant botanical illustrations blended with modern research aesthetics.

Include sections:

1. Research Portfolio Intelligence
2. Ethics & CTRI Readiness
3. Participant Lifecycle Management
4. Pharmacovigilance & SAE Monitoring
5. Compliance & Audit Trail
6. Standards Export (FHIR & CDISC)
7. Explainable Risk Detection

Add smooth premium scroll animations.

After entering platform:

Use TOP NAVIGATION instead of a large sidebar.

Navigation:
Portfolio

Show:

Use elegant cards and layouts with strong visual hierarchy.

Do NOT show dozens of metrics.

ANIMATIONS

Use:
- smooth transitions
- subtle hover effects
- elegant loading states
- premium micro-interactions

Avoid:
- flashy effects
- excessive motion
- gimmicky animations

FINAL GOAL

The entire product should look like a ₹50 lakh+ enterprise platform built for AIIA and national clinical research institutions.

Every screen should feel intentional, premium, modern, trustworthy and production-ready.
